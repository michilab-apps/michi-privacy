Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

🔒 TL;DR - Quick Summary

Michi is a privacy-first app designed to keep your focus on your goals. Your plans, goals, tasks, sessions, and reflection notes are stored privately on your physical device. We only transmit data to third-party services when you explicitly create an AI plan, format notes, manage a Pro subscription, or when anonymous diagnostic crash logs are collected to maintain app stability.

1. On-Device Data Storage

Your data (Plans, Goals, Tasks, Sessions, and Reflection Notes) is stored locally on your device. We do not inspect, collect, or sell your plans, session history, or personal notes.

Your personal data stays privately on your device. External transmission occurs only in the following transparent scenarios:

  • You explicitly use "Generate with AI" or "Format Notes" (routed to vetted AI providers under strict Zero Data Retention).
  • You purchase or restore a Michi Pro subscription (processed anonymously through RevenueCat and your platform's app store: Apple App Store or Google Play).
  • An unexpected error occurs (anonymized crash traces collected by Sentry to fix bugs).

2. AI Data Processing (Apple 5.1.2i & Google Play Disclosure)

⚠️ Third-Party AI Data Processing & Zero Data Retention

When you explicitly invoke AI plan generation or note formatting, Michi transmits your plan topic, experience level, optional context, or the note text you choose to format through secure HTTPS to vetted AI providers via OpenRouter:

  • AI Providers: OpenAI (via Microsoft Azure), Meta Llama (via Groq), Mistral AI (EU Sovereign Cloud), Google Gemini (via Google Cloud Vertex AI), and DeepSeek (via OpenRouter)
  • Purpose: Generate customized sequential stages, goals, actionable tasks, and format reflection notes
  • Zero Data Retention (ZDR) Guarantee: All AI requests are routed exclusively to endpoints that enforce strict Zero Data Retention (zdr: true) and training denial (data_collection: "deny"). Your prompts are never used to train AI models
  • Security in Transit: All communications use TLS/HTTPS encryption

Your consent is required. Before your first AI generation, Michi presents an explicit consent modal that:

  • Identifies the AI providers and transmission channels by name
  • Explains what data will be processed and why
  • Allows you to decline without losing core app functionality (you can create and track plans manually)
  • Provides an instant toggle to revoke AI consent anytime in Settings

3. Diagnostics, Billing & Cloud Infrastructure

To ensure app reliability and deliver subscription entitlements, Michi integrates with trusted, privacy-conscious service providers:

  • Sentry: Anonymized crash logs and device diagnostics (such as OS version and device model) to detect errors and improve performance. No personal plans, notes, or identifiable details are collected.
  • RevenueCat & App Store Billing: Handles purchase verification and subscription receipts (via Apple App Store or Google Play) using an anonymous app user ID. No financial or credit card data is accessible to Michi.
  • Supabase: Stateless cloud edge functions that proxy AI requests and enforce fair rate limits without persisting your plan contents or reflection notes.

4. What We Don't Do

  • ❌ We do not sell your personal data or personal progress history to third parties
  • ❌ We do not use your personal notes or goals to train AI models
  • ❌ We do not track your behavior across other apps or websites
  • ❌ We do not show you third-party advertisements
  • ❌ We do not require a mandatory cloud account to use the app

5. Data Retention & Deletion

Local Data: All your plan data remains on your physical device until you delete the app or use the "Reset all data" feature in Settings.

Database Export: You can export and import a complete, human-readable JSON backup of your database anytime from Settings.

AI & Diagnostic Logs: For error monitoring and rate limiting, stateless anonymous counters and truncated diagnostic logs (first 100 characters) are retained for up to 30 days and automatically purged.

6. Children's Privacy

Michi is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last Updated" date and posting the revised policy within the app.
