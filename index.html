Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

🔒 TL;DR - Quick Summary

Michi is a privacy-first app designed to keep your focus on your goals. Your plans, goals, tasks, sessions, and reflection notes are stored privately on your physical device. We only transmit data to third-party services when you explicitly create an AI plan, format notes, manage a Pro subscription, or when anonymous diagnostic crash logs are collected to maintain app stability.

1. On-Device Data Storage

Your data (Plans, Goals, Tasks, Sessions, and Reflection Notes) is stored locally on your device. We do not inspect, collect, or sell your plans, session history, or personal notes.

Your personal data stays privately on your device. External transmission occurs only in the following transparent scenarios:

  • You explicitly use "Generate with AI" or "Format Notes" (routed to vetted AI providers under strict Zero Data Retention).
  • You purchase or restore a Michi Pro subscription (processed anonymously through RevenueCat and your platform's app store: Apple App Store or Google Play).
  • An unexpected error occurs (anonymized crash traces collected by Sentry to fix bugs).

2. AI Plan Generation & Note Formatting

When you choose to use our optional AI features, we transmit minimal context to structure your curriculum or tidy your reflections:

  • Curriculum Planning: Your plan title, experience level, and optional pacing context.
  • Session Reflection Formatting: The raw text notes you choose to structure.

🤖 AI Provider Privacy & Zero Data Retention Guarantees

We route requests through vetted industry AI providers (OpenAI GPT-6 Luna, Meta Llama 3.3 via Groq, Mistral AI, Google Gemini, and DeepSeek via OpenRouter) under enterprise agreements featuring Zero Data Retention (ZDR):

  • No Model Training: Your prompts, goals, and reflections are never used to train or fine-tune public AI models.
  • Stateless Execution: Data is processed in transient memory to generate the response and is not stored by AI providers beyond the immediate request-response window.
  • Explicit Voluntary Consent: Per Apple Guideline 5.1.2(i) and GDPR standards, you are presented with an explicit consent modal identifying third-party AI processors before your first generation. You can revoke this permission anytime in Settings.

3. Third-Party Infrastructure & Diagnostics

To deliver a fast, reliable, and secure experience, we work with industry-standard infrastructure providers:

  • Supabase: Serves as our stateless backend gateway to route AI requests and verify rate limits. No personal plan data is stored in cloud databases.
  • RevenueCat: Validates app store receipts for Michi Pro subscriptions. We do not receive or store your credit card or financial details.
  • Sentry: Collects anonymous crash logs, device OS versions, and stack traces to fix unexpected errors. Stack traces do not contain your personal notes.

4. No Advertising & No Data Sales

Our business model is simple: we sell software, not your data.

  • ❌ We do not sell your personal data or session history to data brokers
  • ❌ We do not track your behavior across other apps or websites
  • ❌ We do not show you third-party advertisements
  • ❌ We do not require a mandatory cloud account to use the app

5. Data Retention & Deletion

Local Data: All your plan data remains on your physical device until you delete the app or use the "Reset all data" feature in Settings.

Database Export: You can export and import a complete, human-readable JSON backup of your database anytime from Settings.

AI & Diagnostic Logs: For error monitoring and rate limiting, stateless anonymous counters and truncated diagnostic logs (first 100 characters) are retained for up to 30 days and automatically purged.

6. Children's Privacy

Michi is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last Updated" date and posting the revised policy within the app.
